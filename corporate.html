About me

I am an oceanographer and numerical modeller. I work on how water moves in coastal and offshore systems — hydrodynamics, waves, pollutant dispersion and, more recently, flood risk — and on turning model results into information for licensing and engineering decisions.

Over fifteen years I have worked on environmental licensing studies for Brazilian offshore basins, on a Lagrangian drift model that became the SPAD-SAR search-and-rescue R&D programme, on the Costa Norte project on mangrove vulnerability to oil spills, and on metocean criteria and wave hindcasts at Fugro in Dubai (2023–2024).

I am Modelling Coordinator at Umi San in Brazil and a PhD candidate in Environmental Engineering at UFES, developing a probabilistic framework for flood risk assessment in data-scarce watersheds.
